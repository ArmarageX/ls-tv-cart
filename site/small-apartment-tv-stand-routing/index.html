FAQ

Does this page link to the product roundup?

No. It supports the main rolling TV stand guide and focuses on setup details.

What is the first safety check?

Match the TV size and weight to the stand rating and follow the product instructions.

Should wheels lock?

Locking casters are useful when the screen is parked for viewing.

What about cables?

Plan cable slack, ties, and power access before rolling the stand through a room.

Is a shelf necessary?

Only if you need a streaming device, laptop, camera, or small console close to the screen.
